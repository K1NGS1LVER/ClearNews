# ClearNews — MISSION.md

## Why this project exists

ClearNews is a news-tracking system built end-to-end to answer a question most newsfeeds don't: **how does a story actually develop over time**, across outlets, across political leanings, and across language?

Most news products show you today's front page. ClearNews instead tracks individual stories — clustering articles together, measuring how coverage evolves (volume, sentiment, narrative drift, political composition), and letting you ask questions about what happened and when.

## What the user wants out of it

The user is learning about this project — specifically:
- The **flow** of the system: how data enters, gets processed, and ends up on screen
- **Why** each architectural decision was made the way it was
- The **pros and cons** of each major choice

This mission will be served by a sequence of lessons that walk through the architecture, the NLP/ML pipeline decisions, the retrieval and agent design, and the trade-offs at each stage.
